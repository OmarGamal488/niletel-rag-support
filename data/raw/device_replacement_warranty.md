Warranty Periods:

| Device Category | Warranty | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Router (FTTH) | 24 شهر | Manufacturing defects, software bugs |
| MiFi / 5G CPE | 12 شهر | Hardware failures, battery defect |
| Mobile phones (bundled) | 12 شهر | Per manufacturer terms |
| STB | 12 شهر | Hardware + remote control |
| Accessories (cables, power adapters) | 6 شهور | Only original failures |

What's Covered:
- Manufacturing defects in materials/workmanship
- Software-only issues solvable via firmware
- Battery degradation > 30% in first 12 months
- Power surge damage IF NileTel surge protector was installed
- Failure during normal use within specifications

What's NOT Covered:
- Physical damage (drops, water, etc.)
- Liquid damage (visible from indicator chip)
- Unauthorized modifications (rooting, jailbreaking, hardware mods)
- Damage from third-party power equipment (e.g., faulty UPS)
- Cosmetic wear and tear
- Lost or stolen devices (file police report → insurance claim if covered)

Claim Process:

**Step 1: Initial Troubleshooting**
- Agent runs remote diagnostics via TR-069
- 60% of "broken device" calls are actually:
  - Wrong password configurations
  - Need firmware update
  - Cable issues
  - Power adapter failure (cheaper to replace just adapter)

**Step 2: In-Store Diagnosis** (لو الـ remote ما حلش)
- العميل يجيب الجهاز للـ branch الأقرب
- Technician يفحصه live
- يكتب diagnostic report
- لو covered: replacement تلقائي
- لو not covered: quote للـ paid repair

**Step 3: Replacement**
- Same model لو متاح
- Equivalent model لو الـ original out of stock
- Free upgrade لو الجهاز اللي عند العميل deprecated (e.g., HG8245H to OptiXstar)
- العميل بياخد الـ replacement في 30 دقيقة من الـ branch
- Old device بيرجع NileTel للـ refurbishment program

**Step 4: Home Delivery** (للـ VIP + Corporate)
- Replacement device delivered to customer location
- Technician installs و verifies
- No store visit needed
- Available 8 AM - 8 PM, 7 أيام في الأسبوع

Customer-Owned Devices (BYOD):
- NileTel allows certified third-party routers
- List available at niletel.com.eg/compatible-devices
- Limited support: provisioning + ACS access only
- No warranty support من NileTel side
- Manufacturer warranty applies

Damaged/Lost Device Charges:
لو العميل بيطلب replacement بسبب damage مش covered:
- FTTH router: 350-800 جنيه (حسب الـ model)
- 5G CPE: 1,200-2,500 جنيه
- MiFi: 600-900 جنيه
- STB: 500 جنيه
- Insurance plan متاحة: 30 جنيه/شهر تغطي accidental damage (deductible 100 جنيه)

End-of-Life Devices:
لما الـ device يـ reach end-of-service من الـ manufacturer:
- NileTel offers free upgrade for active customers
- Notification 6 شهور مسبقاً
- Mass campaign in regions affected
- Old devices recycled through certified e-waste programs

Recycling Program:
- العميل يقدر يجيب أي electronics قديمة (مش بس NileTel devices)
- خصم 10% على باقته الشهر اللي بعده
- شهادة تخلص responsible من الـ NileTel sustainability program
- Partnership مع Ministry of Environment

Common Issues & Quick Solutions:

"Router مش بيشتغل" → Power adapter check first (50% of cases). New adapter بـ 50 جنيه vs full replacement.

"Wi-Fi مفصول كل ساعة" → Firmware update (70% of cases solve)، أو RF interference

"MiFi بطارية تنفد بسرعة" → لو > 18 شهر: out of warranty. لو < 12: free replacement.

"STB مش بيشغل التطبيقات" → Storage full (مش hardware)، factory reset
