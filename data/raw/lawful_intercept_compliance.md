Lawful Intercept & Legal Disclosure Compliance

التعامل مع طلبات الـ lawful intercept والإفصاح القانوني

يا فندم، الـ NileTel ملتزمة بـ Law 175/2018 (anti-cybercrime law) و Communications Regulation Act 10/2003. أي طلب من جهات حكومية لازم يتعامل معاه بحرفية كاملة. الـ agent العادي ما يتعاملش مع الطلبات دي مباشرة — الـ escalation فوراً لـ Legal & Compliance Office.

Authorized Requesting Authorities:
- النيابة العامة (Public Prosecution)
- وزارة الداخلية (Ministry of Interior) — بـ court order
- الأمن الوطني (National Security Authority)
- جهاز المخابرات (General Intelligence)
- NTRA (NTRA Telecom Regulatory Authority)

Types of Requests:

**Type A: Real-Time Intercept**
- Live monitoring لمكالمات / SMS / data of specific subscriber
- يتطلب court order ساري لمدة محددة (max 90 يوم)
- يتم عبر dedicated LI infrastructure (mandated by NTRA)
- لا أحد في الـ customer-facing teams بيكون عنده visibility

**Type B: Subscriber Data Disclosure**
- KYC info, billing records, traffic logs (metadata only)
- يحتاج formal written request + case reference number
- Response in 5 days for routine, 24 hours for urgent

**Type C: Location Data**
- Real-time or historical cell tower location
- Court order required
- Response in 1 hour for emergency, 24 hours otherwise

**Type D: Network Suspension**
- Block specific subscriber, SIM, or IMEI
- NTRA order or court order
- Immediate (within 30 minutes)

What Customer Care Agents Must Do:

**If a customer asks "did anyone request my data?"**
- Answer: "يا فندم، NileTel ما تفصحش عن أي طلبات قانونية. ده compliance issue لازم تتواصل مع الـ Legal Department على legal@niletel.com.eg."
- لا تـ confirm أو تـ deny

**If a customer says "the police asked you for my info, what did you give?"**
- Same response above
- Do NOT discuss any specifics
- Document the conversation in CRM

**If you receive a call/email claiming to be police/military/intelligence asking for customer info**:
- DO NOT provide ANY information by phone or email
- Politely respond: "يا فندم، NileTel بتلتزم بالـ formal channels فقط. الطلبات لازم تتقدم رسمياً من خلال [proper authority] لـ legal@niletel.com.eg."
- Document the call + escalate to Compliance immediately
- Many social engineering attempts come this way

**If you receive a written court order or NTRA letter**:
- Forward immediately to Compliance Office (compliance@niletel.com.eg)
- DO NOT take any action without Compliance sign-off
- Log it in the LI Tracker

Customer Notification:
- معظم الـ LI orders بتيجي مع gag order — ممنوع notification للعميل
- في بعض الـ subscriber data requests (non-confidential)، NileTel بتـ notify العميل بعد 90 يوم
- ال policy detail في الـ Privacy Policy v3.2 (April 2026)

Privacy & Data Minimization:
NileTel ما بتـ retain أكتر من المطلوب قانوناً:
- Voice / SMS metadata: 12 شهر (per NTRA mandate)
- Data traffic logs: 6 شهور
- KYC documents: 2 سنين بعد account closure
- Billing records: 7 سنين (tax law)
- Location data: 30 يوم (للـ non-emergency)
- Anything beyond: automatic deletion

Customer Data Access Requests (CDAR):
العميل العادي يقدر يطلب نسخة من بياناته:
- يـ submit request عبر Self-Care App أو email
- ID verification mandatory
- NileTel بترد في 30 يوم
- نسخة مجانية مرة كل سنة، نسخ إضافية بـ 50 جنيه
- بعض الـ data exempted (security-related, ongoing investigations)

Right to Erasure (Article 17 of Data Protection Law):
- العميل يقدر يطلب delete لبياناته بعد account closure
- Exceptions: data required for legal compliance, ongoing disputes, regulatory reporting
- Response in 30 days
- لو exempted: clear explanation للعميل

International Requests:
- Foreign government requests via mutual legal assistance treaty (MLAT) only
- لا direct response لـ FBI, Interpol, etc.
- All channeled through Egyptian Ministry of Justice

Audit & Reporting:
- Annual transparency report (published on niletel.com.eg)
- Total LI requests received (aggregated, no specifics)
- Number granted vs rejected
- Average response time
- NTRA-mandated quarterly compliance report

Training Requirements:
- كل employee اللي بيتعامل مع customer data بياخد annual compliance training
- Test pass score: 85%+
- Refresher عند update الـ laws
- Specialized training للـ Legal team

Whistleblower Protections:
لو agent شاف internal misconduct related to lawful intercept:
- Internal hotline: ethics@niletel.com.eg
- Anonymous reporting protected by law
- No retaliation policy strict
- External reporting to NTRA Ethics Committee also protected

Penalties for Violations:
- Employee misconduct: termination + criminal prosecution
- Company-level: fines 5-50 million EGP
- License revocation in severe cases
